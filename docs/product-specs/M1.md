# M1

A person chooses Ada, creates Northwind, qualifies that contact, and sends an estimate whose page shows `This is a test.`

## Outcome and actor

The actor is the acting user chosen from the dropdown. There is no password. The outcome is scenario 1: create a contact, qualify it, and send the estimate email. The kanban card is the contact. `column` is a property of the contact.

## Done-means

- Choosing Ada and creating a contact named Northwind shows that contact in the column `new`.
- Filling email `northwind@example.com` and company `Northwind` and clicking Validate shows the column `qualified`.
- An estimate with Cable quantity 2 and Lamp quantity 1 shows grand total 25. Validating it sends the email, shows the column `estimate sent`, and shows the text `This is a test.`
- Submitting an empty name shows the code `contact.name.required` and does not add a contact.
- Sending an estimate for a qualified contact whose email is empty shows the code `contact.email.required`, leaves the column `qualified`, and records no email.
- A state-changing action with no acting user shows the code `user.required` and writes no row.
- Northwind belongs to Ada. Ada sees it on the kanban, the contact list, and, after the send, on the estimate list. Ben sees none of those rows. Cable and Lamp stay available to either user.

## Non-goals

Speech, a microphone, an LLM orchestrator, passwords, login, Fortify, a fancy email view, decorative effects, payments, attachments, teams, API tokens, a second mail channel, PDF, CSV, counts, uc32, user CRUD, product CRUD screens, and estimate CRUD beyond creating an estimate in scenario 1. Pagination has no page-size integer in this proof of concept.

## Assumptions, each confirmed or guess

- confirmed: the four objects are contact, product, estimate, and user. User is name only.
- confirmed: columns are `new`, `qualified`, and `estimate sent`.
- confirmed: the email body is `This is a test.` and it is shown as plain text on the contact.
- confirmed: storage is PostgreSQL and the application connection is `pgsql`. `login-mechanism` is none.
- Steward rule, recorded in `docs/intent-questions.md` and `docs/exec-plans/decisions.md`: a contact belongs to the acting user who creates it. The kanban, the contact list, and the estimate list are restricted to that user. Products stay shared. The selected user id is stored in the session.
- POC choice: one contact may have more than one estimate. The first successful send sets `estimate sent`. Later sends leave it there.
- POC choice: qualify may store an empty email and still set `qualified` when the column was `new`. A later qualify does not move `estimate sent` backwards.
- POC choice: mail delivery is Laravel's log mailer, and a sent-email row stores the to address and the body.

## Invariants this slice must not break

- A contact is created with column `new` and belongs to the acting user who created it.
- Qualify from `new` sets column `qualified`.
- The first successful send sets column `estimate sent`. A later successful send leaves that column.
- Grand total is the sum of quantity times that product's unit price. Cable 10 times quantity 2, plus Lamp 5 times quantity 1, is 25.
- The sent body is exactly `This is a test.`
- Empty name writes no contact. Empty email on send writes no estimate and no email. Missing acting user writes no row.
- The CRM log does not include the email address or the body.

## Data written and data read, with protection level

Written: user name; contact name, email, company, column, and the user the contact belongs to; product name and unit price; estimate contact, lines of product and whole-number quantity, and grand total; sent email to address and body `This is a test.`

Read on the kanban and the contact list: the acting user's contact name and column. Read on the estimate list: that user's contact name and grand total. Read on the contact page: name, email, company, column, grand total, and `This is a test.` after a send. The dropdown reads user names. Products are read by every acting user.

Protection level of each field: not named.

## Security-critical paths touched, or "none"

The acting-user session, the Policy check that refuses a write when no user is selected, the Policy check that refuses another user's contact, and the contact email that is stored and shown on the contact page. There is no password and no login route.

## New dependencies, or "none"

## Acceptance examples

- Given Ada is the acting user, when they create a contact named Northwind, then Northwind is in the kanban column `new`.
- Given Ada is the acting user, when they submit a contact with an empty name, then the code is `contact.name.required` and no contact row is written.
- Given the contact Northwind, when they fill email `northwind@example.com` and company `Northwind` and click Validate, then the column is `qualified`.
- Given that qualified contact, when they add Cable at unit price 10 with quantity 2 and Lamp at unit price 5 with quantity 1 and click Validate estimate, then the grand total is 25, the column is `estimate sent`, and the page shows `This is a test.`
- Given a qualified contact whose email is empty, when they send Cable quantity 2 and Lamp quantity 1, then the code is `contact.email.required`, the column stays `qualified`, and no email is recorded.
- Given no acting user, when they submit a contact named Northwind, then the code is `user.required` and no contact row is written.
- Given Ada created Northwind and sent the estimate, when Ben is the acting user, then the kanban, the contact list, and the estimate list do not show Northwind. When Ada is the acting user, those pages show Northwind. Cable and Lamp remain on the contact page.
- Given Northwind is already `estimate sent`, when Ada sends another estimate, then the column stays `estimate sent` and the body is still `This is a test.`

## Test seams

- Domain: `CreateContact`, `QualifyContact`, and `SendEstimate` called with an in-memory repository. Middleware is absent. This catches the column words, the empty-name refusal, the empty-email refusal, the grand total 25, and the body `This is a test.` It misses HTTP status and the page.
- HTTP: the store, qualify, and estimate routes via `php artisan test`, on the sqlite test database. This catches status, error code, and whether a row exists. It misses the browser.
- Journey: Playwright against the origin `init.sh` prints. This catches the visible steps. It misses a second estimate and a missing acting user unless a later case adds them.

## Quality budgets that apply, or "unchanged"

Unchanged. Every constitution budget is `unset`.

## Risk class

The acting-user session and the stored contact email, because a write depends on the session and the contact page shows the email.

## How a failure is rolled back
